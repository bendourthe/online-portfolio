I specialize in building and scaling intelligent systems, from first-in-class wearables and implantables to advanced imaging solutions, guiding them from initial prototype to clinical-grade product.

My technical foundation combines deep expertise in Algorithm Development, Signal Processing, Data Science, Computer Vision and Machine Learning, with a strong focus on engineering discipline. I define myself by fast, robust iteration and cross-functional leadership. Whether aligning software engineering with clinical goals or automating complex data pipelines, I am passionate about bridging the gap between cutting-edge research and accessible, life-saving technologies.

Nexus-Hub

From agentic AI to an autonomous team of world experts

Codex, Claude Code, Gemini, Cursor, and other agentic systems already bring powerful planning, coding, and tool-use abilities. Nexus-Hub upgrades those platforms with an advanced harness of multidisciplinary field experts, repeatable workflows, review gates, and project conventions that make the work more reliable across tools, teams, and sessions.

It does not replace your assistant. It gives that assistant structured access to senior engineering disciplines: architecture, security, testing, documentation, product thinking, and delivery practice, all coordinated around the same goal and definition of done.
